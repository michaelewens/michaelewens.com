Invitation-Only Workshop

Expanding Access to Private Markets

Date Thursday, July 30, 2026
Time 9:00 AM – 1:15 PM EDT
Location Kravis Hall 680 · Columbia Business School
Organizers Michael Ewens (Columbia) & Ben Bates (BYU Law)

A small workshop bringing together financial economists, legal academics, and industry participants on the push to bring retail investors into private markets.

Schedule

Run of Show
9:00 – 9:15

Welcome & Framing

Michael Ewens (Columbia Business School) and Ben Bates (BYU Law)

9:15 – 10:30

Part I: Academic Presentations

Renée Jones (Boston College Law School), Will Clayton (BYU Law), and Elisabeth de Fontenay (Duke Law School) present the skeptical view of the expansion. Michael Ewens and Ben Bates present the pro case. Papers pre-circulated; each talk 15 minutes plus open discussion.

10:30 – 10:50

Break

10:50 – 12:00

Part II: Premortem Panel

Greg Brown (UNC Kenan-Flagler), Drew Carrington (iCapital), and Jeffrey Gordon (Columbia Law School).

“Imagine retail investors gain widespread access to private markets, and it ends badly: a run, a valuation dispute, a conflict of interest, or something else. What went wrong? Why? And what should we have been watching?”

12:00 – 12:15

Wrap-Up & Research Agenda

Organizers collect ideas and open questions; circulated to participants afterward.

12:15 – 1:15

Working Lunch
